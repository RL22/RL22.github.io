# Product marketing context

Reusable positioning and copy guidance for Rodney L. Lewis's portfolio and Sprintz studio. Use this document when writing or reviewing portfolio, case-study, outreach, or conversion copy.

## Primary outcome

Help a recruiter, hiring manager, or marketing executive understand that Rodney is a hands-on senior operator who owns marketing-web platforms end to end, then invite them to book a 20-minute introduction or start a conversation.

The portfolio is also a Sprintz studio business card. Prospective clients should see a senior operator who can sell, design, engineer, and ship—not a generic freelance service.

## Audiences

- Senior IC marketing-web roles: Vanta, Hightouch, PandaDoc, DoorDash, and similar SaaS, fintech, or AI companies.
- Recruiters, hiring managers, marketing leaders, and engineering leaders evaluating platform ownership.
- Prospective Sprintz clients evaluating strategic judgment and craft.

## Positioning pillars

1. **The website as a growth product.** A high-velocity pipeline engine that connects marketing, product, engineering, analytics, and acquisition.
2. **Hands-on senior IC ownership.** Architecture, performance, components, publishing workflows, and automated release gates—not just ticket delivery.
3. **Headless CMS at scale.** Experience with headless WordPress, Sanity, and decoupled component systems that increase content velocity.
4. **Performance, technical SEO, and AI discoverability.** Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse-driven refactors, JSON-LD governance, and accurate product claims for AI search.
5. **Reusable systems with business outcomes.** Modular templates at Carrot reduced marketing development requests by 30% — self-reported, confined to one field on the live site (`carrot-cms-architecture`'s `outcome`), not the lead proof. Lead instead with durability a stranger can verify without trusting Rodney: a Mednition page still live and unchanged years after a five-month contract, Pendo's URL architecture still in production, Carrot's careers page still rendering live from the Greenhouse API.
6. **Migration leadership.** WordPress-to-headless and Marketo-to-HubSpot migrations for enterprise marketing teams.
7. **AI-native delivery.** Next.js, Sanity, Supabase, Playwright, AI-assisted development, and automated release gates.
8. **Builder and strategist.** A solo studio founder who can connect business goals to technical execution.

## Voice

- Professional, peer-to-peer, collegial, and quietly senior.
- Specific and confident; show the artifact or outcome instead of adding adjectives.
- Prefer ownership verbs: *own, lead, partner, architect, ship*.
- Prefer concrete artifacts: roadmaps, component libraries, publishing workflows, release gates, and exact metrics.
- Avoid *passionate*, *innovative*, *streamline*, *synergy*, generic “developer who loves…” language, and agency hype.
- Reference voice: Stripe documentation and Linear product copy—plain, precise, and useful.

## Verification rules

- Plain text claims must be directly verifiable in the resume or an explicitly named source.
- Italicized claims are positioning research or user-provided context and require review before publication.
- Never fabricate numbers, testimonials, company names, or technical experience.
- Use the exact public metric where it appears: **30% fewer marketing dev requests at Carrot**. Never rewrite it as "30% faster delivery." It is user-asserted, not on either resume of record, and confined to one field, one case study — never repeated elsewhere and never the lead claim.

## Objections to answer

- **Specific CMS experience:** Frame Sanity, Contentful, and headless WordPress as transferable composable-CMS patterns.
- **Roadmap ownership:** Show multi-quarter platform work at Sprintz, Carrot, and Pendo rather than isolated tickets.
- **Marketing and engineering bridge:** Show partnerships with demand generation, SEO, legal/compliance, and product teams.
- **Management depth:** Describe the Revel management experience accurately and pair it with senior IC platform leadership.
- **Evidence:** Lead with durability proof a stranger can verify (Mednition, Pendo's URL architecture, Carrot's Greenhouse integration); the Carrot percentage is confirmatory, not the opener.
