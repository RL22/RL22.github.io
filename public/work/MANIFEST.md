# Work assets manifest

Web-ready case-study figures captured from the Internet Archive Wayback Machine and live verified environments.

**Method.** Pages were loaded from Wayback replay URLs (`/web/<timestamp>/<url>`) or live verified campaign endpoints in headless Chromium at high-resolution desktop viewports (1600px width). Before screenshotting, the Wayback replay banner (`#wm-ipp-base`, `#wm-ipp`), cookie-consent overlays (OneTrust, Cookiebot), HubSpot interactive anchors, and floating widgets (Drift, Intercom) were removed. Consent was accepted or suppressed, margin-top was reset, and font loading was awaited via `document.fonts.ready`. Broken 0-width tracking pixels were suppressed. Images capture the semantic top or container region of each page.

**Format.** Every asset ships as an optimized 256-colour PNG and a WebP at quality 85. All files satisfy the performance budget (WebP $\le 150$ KB, PNG $\le 300$ KB). Prefer the WebP with the PNG as fallback.

**Serving path.** `/work/<filename>`.
