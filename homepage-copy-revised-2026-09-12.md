## 4. How I Think (Core Principles)

### Section Eyebrow
`Principles`

### Headline
**Systems that compound instead of decay.**

### 01 Build systems that compound
Every architectural choice either strengthens the platform or creates future drag. I design modular component libraries and clean content models that become more valuable as your team grows.

### 02 Scale delivery with human gates
AI powers rapid technical execution, but unguided speed creates tech debt. Senior humans guide architecture at the start, AI accelerates the build, and human testing enforces the release gate.

### 03 Make marketing self-sufficient
Success is not just launch day. The real test of a platform is how long your marketing team can launch pages, test messaging, and run campaigns without filing an engineering ticket.

### 04 Turn pages into live experiments
A marketing site exists to drive pipeline, not just display layout. I pre-wire event tracking and variant testing directly into UI components, so data guides every iteration.

### Section Callout
The best web platforms make everyone around them faster. That is the entire job.
