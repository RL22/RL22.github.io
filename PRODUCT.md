# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening for Senior IC marketing-web roles (active targets: Vanta "Senior Web Developer", Hightouch "Web Engineer", PandaDoc "Senior Web Developer", DoorDash "Front-End Web Developer, B2B MarTech"). They arrive from a resume or LinkedIn, spend 30–90 seconds scanning, and are deciding one thing: is this person a hands-on senior engineer who owns marketing web platforms end-to-end? Secondary reader: a prospective Sprintz studio client evaluating craft.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Rodney L. Lewis (rl22.github.io). One conversion event: the reader books an intro call or submits the contact form. The site itself is the primary work sample; its craft, performance, and accessibility are proof of the claims it makes. Success: a screener leaves convinced Rodney operates at senior platform-owner level and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Confident craftsman. Calm, precise, quietly senior. Warm and human (terracotta/cream, real photography) but engineering-first in voice: outcomes over adjectives, ownership verbs not management verbs. Never salesy, never shouty.

**Metric policy.** The site is metric-free apart from two sanctioned claims: the tenure figure ("nine years owning marketing-site lifecycles", scoped to Oct 2016 onward), and "reduced marketing dev requests by 30%", which appears once, in the outcome of the `carrot-cms-architecture` case study. No other quantified claim may be introduced anywhere. `app/work/content.test.ts` enforces this for `/work`; see the revision notes in `SITE_COPY.md` for the full history, including the 2026-07-21 removal this partially supersedes.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev-portfolio templates (Hero/About/Skills/Contact filled in with stock patterns).
- SaaS clichés: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-heading-text card grids, gradient text.
- Dark-mode-by-default developer/terminal aesthetic (neon on black).
- Marketing-agency hype tone; anything that reads "growth hacker" rather than "platform engineer".

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the work sample.** Every section must demonstrate the craft it claims: performance, accessibility, typographic care.
2. **Scan-first structure.** A screener gets the argument in 30 seconds; depth rewards the reader who stays.
3. **One claim, one number, everywhere.** Consistency of the 30% metric and ownership language is credibility.
4. **Warm restraint.** Committed terracotta accents on cream; personality through warmth and precision, not volume.
5. **Practice what you preach.** WCAG AA, reduced motion respected, real semantic structure: the portfolio of a platform owner behaves like a platform.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Contrast-checked text on cream/terracotta surfaces, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for all reveal/float animations, semantic heading hierarchy, keyboard-reachable interactive elements.
